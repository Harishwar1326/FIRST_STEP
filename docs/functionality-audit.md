## Data incident during audit

An unauthenticated `POST /api/v1/lessons/import` was mistakenly sent to the already-running backend on port 5000 before verifying that its loaded code matched the working tree. That process started at 10:49 local time, before the route guard and additive seeder were edited at 11:07 and 11:03. The request returned 200 and ran the stale public importer. Read-only inspection of the local Mongo database found seven bundled lessons, four bundled quizzes, and three bundled tasks, all recreated at 05:44:51 UTC. Whether earlier custom curriculum existed and was deleted cannot be determined from current records. No backup/dump artifact or `mongodump` executable was found in the workspace. No restore was attempted because a source snapshot was unavailable. The stale process was stopped after the sandbox denied the initial stop request and escalated execution was approved.
